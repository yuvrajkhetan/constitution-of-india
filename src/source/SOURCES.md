# Official sources

Everything the site's text and history rest on, saved here so it does not depend on a website staying up. Collected 24 to 28 November 2026.

## The current text

- `Constitution_of_India_10.9.2026.pdf`: *The Constitution of India [As on 10th September, 2026]*, Legislative Department (English half of the diglot edition). `...pdf.txt` is its text copy.

## The original text, 1949 (in force 26 January 1950)

- `original_1949/Gazette_of_India_1949-11-26_Constitution.pdf`: the Gazette of India Extraordinary, 26 November 1949, Constituent Assembly of India Notification No. CA/83/Cons./49, publishing the Constitution as passed and authenticated (Gazette pages 2347 to 2597, 254 PDF pages). Originally egazette.nic.in `WriteReadData/1949/E-2358-1949-0000-109779.pdf`; this copy from https://archive.org/details/TheConstitutionOfIndia1949 . No amendment came into force before the First Amendment of 1951, so this is the text of 26 January 1950. Its text layer has about 1% word errors (old-style figures, side notes mixed in): proofread against the page images before use.
- A second official printing for cross-checking (not saved, 26 MB): Government of India Press, https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.211432 , same type and page breaks.

## The Amendment Acts

`acts/NNN.pdf` is the official text of the Constitution (NNN Amendment) Act. Where one Gazette carries several Acts, the same file is saved under each number. `acts/compilation_LS2008.pdf` is *Constitution Amendment in India*, 7th edition, Lok Sabha Secretariat, February 2008 (from https://archive.org/download/eparlib.nic.in.63/Constitution_Amendment_India_LS_2008.pdf), which reprints Acts 1 to 94 in full (Annexure A, PDF pages 367 to 678). It is retypeset and has its own typos, so prefer the Gazette copy where there is one.
