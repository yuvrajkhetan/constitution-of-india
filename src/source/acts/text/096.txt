THE CONSTITUTION (NINETY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 2011
[23rd September, 2011.]

1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Ninety-sixth Amendment) Act, 2011.

2. In the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution, in entry 15, for the word "Oriya", the word "Odia" shall be substituted.
