         MINISTRY OF LAW, JUSTICE AND COMPANY AFFAIRS
                                   (Legislative Department)
                     New Delhi, the 21st January, 2000/Magha 1, 1921 (Saka)

       The following Act of Parliament, after having been ratified by the Legislatures of not
less than one-half of the States by resolutions to that effect, received the assent of the President
on the 21st January, 2000, and is hereby published for general information:—

         THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-NINTH AMENDMENT)
                            ACT, 1999
                                                                            [21st January, 2000.]
                      An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.
      BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fiftieth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
      1. (/) This Act may be called the Constitution (Seventy-ninth Amendment) Act, 1999.              short title and commence-ment
      (2) It shall come into force on the 25th day of January, 2000.
      2. In article 334 of the Constitution, for the words "fifty years", the words "sixty             Amendment of
years" shall be substituted.                                                                           article 334.
