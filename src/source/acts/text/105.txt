THE CONSTITUTION (ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
[18th August, 2021.]

1. (1) This Act may be called the Constitution (One Hundred and Fifth Amendment) Act, 2021.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
[Handwritten on the copy: 15.9.2021, vide notification No. S.O. 4063(E) dated 15.9.2021, w.e.f.]
