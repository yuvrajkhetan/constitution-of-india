                    New Delhi, the 22nd February 1955

     The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on
the 22nd February, 1955 and is hereby published for general information:—

     THE CONSTITUTION (THIRD AMENDMENT)
                    ACT, 1954
                                                   {22nd February, 1955]
       An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.
   BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifth Year of the Republic of
India as follows: —
  1. Short title.—This Act may be called the Constitution (Third
Amendment) Act, 1954.
   2, Amendment of the Seventh Schedule.—In the Seventh Sche-
dule to the Constitution, for entry 33 of List III, the following
entry shall be substituted, namely:—
         "33. Trade and commerce in, and the production, supply and
    distribution of.—
            (a) the products of any industry where the control of
        such industry by the Union is declared by Parliament by law
        to be expedient in the public interest, and imported goods
        of the same kind as such products;

             (b) foodstuffs, including edible oilseeds and oils;
