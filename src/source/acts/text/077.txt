     THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-SEVENTH
           AMENDMENT) ACT, 1995
       An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.
                                                [17th June, 1995]
        BE it enacted by Parliament in the Forty-sixth
        Year of the Republic of India as follows:—
        1. This Act may be called the Constitution Short title.
           (Seventy-seventh Amendment) Act, 1995.
        2. In article 16 of the Constitution after clause Amend-
            (4), the following clause shall be inserted, ment of
                                                          article 16.
            namely:—
            “(4A) Nothing in this article shall prevent
            the State from making any provision for
            reservation in matters of promotion to any
            class or classes of posts in the services under
            the State in favour of the Scheduled Castes
            and the Scheduled Tribes which, in the
            opinion of the State, are not adequately
            represented in the services under the State.”
