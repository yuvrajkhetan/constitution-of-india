THE CONSTITUTION (NINETY-FIFTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 2009
[18th January, 2010.]

1. (1) This Act may be called the Constitution (Ninety-fifth Amendment) Act, 2009.
(2) It shall come into force on the 25th day of January, 2010.

2. In article 334 of the Constitution, for the words "sixty years", the words "seventy years" shall be substituted.
