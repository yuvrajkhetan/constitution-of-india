     MINISTRY OF LAW, JUSTICE AND COMPANY AFFAIRS
                             (Legislative Department)
             New Delhi, the 8th September, 2000/Bhadra 17, 1922 (Saka)
     The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on
8th September, 2000, and is hereby published for general information:—
 THE CONSTITUTION (EIGHTY-THIRD AMENDMENT) ACT, 2000
                                                                      [8th September, 2000.]
               An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.
      BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-first Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
      1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Eighty-third Amendment) Act, 2000.             short title.
      2. In article 243M of the Constitution, after clause (3), the following clause shall be    Amendment of
inserted namely:-                                                                                article 243M.
     "(3A) Nothing in article 243D, relating to reservation of seats for the Scheduled
Castes, shall apply to the State of Arunachal Pradesh.".
