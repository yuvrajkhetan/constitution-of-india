     MINISTRY OF LAW, JUSTICE AND COMPANY AFFAIRS
                              (Legislative Department)
              New Delhi, the 8th September, 2000/Bhadra 17, 1922 (Saka)
     The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on
8th September, 2000, and is hereby published for general information:—
       THE CONSTITUTION (EIGHTY-SECOND AMENDMENT)
                         ACT, 2000
                                                                      [8th September, 2000.]
               An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.
      BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-first year of the Republic of India as follows:—
      1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Eighty-second Amendment) Act, 2000.             short title.
      2. In Article 335 of the Constitution, the following proviso shall be inserted at the       Amendment of
end, namely:—                                                                                     article 335.
            "Provided that nothing in this article shall prevent in making of any provision
      in favour of the members of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes for
      relaxation in qualifying marks in any examination or lowering the standards of
      evaluation, for reservation in matters of promotion to any class or classes of services
      or posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of a State.".
