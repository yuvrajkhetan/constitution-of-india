Source. The Constitution of India [As on 10th September, 2026], Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. Article text, headings and footnotes are reproduced from that edition. Amendment dates and changes are drawn from its footnotes, except for five Acts those footnotes no longer cite; for those we used the Legislative Department's Act texts and the National Portal of India, each linked on the Amendments page.

Plain-English summaries, themes and facts are added to help readers and are not part of the legal text. This is an unofficial reader; for any legal purpose rely on the official publication.
