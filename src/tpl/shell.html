Source. The Constitution of India [As on 10th September, 2026], Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. Article text, headings and official notes are reproduced from that edition. Amendment dates and changes are drawn from its official notes, except for five Acts those notes no longer mention; for those we used the Legislative Department's Act texts and the National Portal of India, each linked on the Amendments page.

Plain-English summaries, themes and facts are added to help readers and are not part of the legal text. This is an unofficial reader; for any legal purpose rely on the official publication.
